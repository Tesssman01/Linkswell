Tools & supplies

Section 1: Disassembly & harness prep

▶VIDEO 02 · High priority
2014–18 GM: tilt lever, column shroud & SWC T-harness

Close-up hands-on: releasing the tilt lever clip with a flathead, splitting the shroud, plugging the SWC T-harness inline.

Over-the-shoulder close-up~2 min
  1. Tilt lever. Pull the tilt lever straight out. Slip a small flathead into the slot between the retainers, twist gently to release the clip, and pull the lever off.
  2. Steering column shroud + SWC harness. Remove the underside shroud screws and pry the halves apart at the seam. Disconnect the main column connector and plug the LinksWell steering wheel control (SWC) T-harness inline between the factory plug and harness.
  3. Microphone. Unclip the driver-side A-pillar trim and peel back the door weatherstrip. Mount the mic near the top of the A-pillar or headliner edge, then route it along the factory loom down into the radio cavity.
  4. Glove box. Remove the 2 top and 2 bottom inner T15 screws, close the door, then remove the 2 lower T15 screws under the frame. Pull the upper and lower glove box structures to reach the factory HMI module on the right wall.

Section 2: Radio removal & bypass

▶VIDEO 03 · High priority
2014–18 GM: HMI module & CD jumper bypass

Show the gray camera plug at the HMI module behind the glove box, installing 'To HMI Connect', and plugging the CD jumper bypass.

Close-up + callout graphics~2 min
  1. Dash trim and screen. Pry off the bezel around the screen and climate panel and unplug its switches (seat buttons, lower switch panel). Remove the four 7 mm screen screws, unplug it, and remove the screen.
  2. CD player bypass. Remove the 7 mm screws on the CD player below the screen and unplug its rear harness. Plug the included flat CD jumper bypass harness into the factory plug to keep the vehicle bus intact.
  3. HMI camera and USB. At the HMI module, unplug the gray camera plug and insert the LinksWell adapter labeled To HMI Connect. Connect the USB extension adapter to the factory USB module. Route both behind the dash into the radio cavity.
Don't skip the CD bypass. Removing the CD player without the jumper breaks bus continuity. Plug it in even though the CD player isn't going back in.

Section 3: Wiring & mounting

  1. Bose retention (if equipped). Connect the factory speaker harness to the Bose Retention Module. Plug the small 2-pin OnStar chime speaker into the main LinksWell harness. Mount both in the lower dash with foam tape to prevent rattles.
  2. Antennas. Lay the GPS, Wi-Fi and dual 4G LTE antennas flat on the inner plastic dash structure under the top windshield panel. Secure extra cable.
  3. Back-panel connections. With the screen face down on a soft pad, connect: dual dark purple LTE, GPS, Wi-Fi, AM/FM antenna adapter; 6-channel RCA (mate Factory Cam In to the HMI video lead); mic jack; main power; SWC lead; and the SIM tray extension routed to the glove box.
  4. Mount and test. Slide the unit in, guiding extra wire down into the lower cavity. Reinstall the four 7 mm screws. Turn the key ON for the first cold boot and confirm the screen lights up.

Screen capture: Car Model AC & CarPlay AutoBoot

Screen capture~90 sec

Module 5 · GM

XL install: 2019–2023 Silverado / Sierra (T1XX)

The biggest job in the course. The T1XX splits the radio into a center display and a separate receiver behind the passenger kick panel, and Bose trucks need the factory amp replaced.

Applies to

2019–2023 Silverado and Sierra 1500/2500/3500 (T1XX chassis).

Labor

About 3–4 hours, including the Bose amp bypass and fiber routing.

Tools

  • 7 mm socket, ratchet and 6" extension
  • 10 mm socket with right-angle swivel adapter
  • T15 Torx driver
  • Nylon trim tools and a panel fastener tool
  • Right-angle pick set: needed for GM secondary connector locks
  • Zip ties and TESA cloth tape

Identify the radio and audio before you start

Factory radioWhat it means
IOS / IOT (high tier)AHD-only backup camera; fiber/digital audio interfaces
IUR (base/mid tier)Supports CVBS or AHD camera signals
Bose 7-channel amplified system? It needs the LinksWell DSP Amp Delete Kit, and the factory amp behind the rear seat gets replaced. Confirm the kit is on hand before booking the job.
Install video: Watch the LinksWell install video for this vehicle at linkswellinc.com/support before your first install.

Phase 1: Dash & console

  1. Console side panels. Unclip the lower console trim. On the passenger side, slide the panel rearward at an angle before pulling, or you'll shear the sliding retention tab.
  2. Console tray. Lift out the tray insert and disconnect the key fob antenna harness underneath. It must be reconnected later.
  3. Center stack trim. Hook under the lower horizontal trim strip with a right-angle pick to release its 5 clips. Unclip the screen bezel and remove the six T15 screws holding the screen frame.
  4. Display out. Pull the screen forward and disconnect the display connectors, HVAC plug and lower switch bank.
  5. Bench transfer. Remove four T15 screws and move the aux switches, USB hubs, trailer brake module, 12V outlets and vents into the LinksWell bezel.

Phase 2: Kick-panel receiver swap

  1. Access. Remove the lower glove box fasteners (7 mm and T15) and the fuse box cover.
  2. Factory receiver out. Use the right-angle 10 mm to remove the two bracket nuts in the kick-panel cavity. Release the locking tabs with a pick and slide the receiver down out of its carriage.
  3. Prep the LinksWell receiver. Attach the vehicle-specific side brackets (IOS/IOT profile).
  4. Antennas. Lay the cellular, GPS and dual Wi-Fi antennas flat on top of the HVAC ducting behind the glove box.
  5. Interconnects. Pass the three main looms (2 display data cables, 1 GM climate extension) from the kick panel up to the center dash.
  6. Connect and install. Plug in main power, AM/FM adapter, chime speaker, AHD camera converter and antennas. Slide the receiver into the carriage until the side clips lock.

Phase 3: Bose amp bypass (if equipped)

  1. Rear seat out. Fold up the rear seat cushion and remove four 10 mm seat frame bolts. Unhook the 3 top latches with a pick and remove the backrest.
  2. Factory amp out. Unbolt the Bose 7-channel amp from the rear cabin wall (10 mm).
  3. DSP amp in. Mount the LinksWell 7-channel DSP amp to its plate and bolt it to the factory studs. Plug the factory Bose connector into the LinksWell bypass T-harness.
  4. Optical and remote. Connect the 5 m right-angle TOSLink optical cable and 5 m bass remote cable. Route both under the passenger door sill carpet to the kick panel.
  5. Turn-on switch. Set the amp's turn-on toggle to Remote In, not Signal Sense.

Phase 4: Mount, test & configure

  1. Mount the display. Attach the top support brackets with the factory T15 screws. Connect the data interconnects and HVAC plug, align the bezel and secure it.
  2. Backup camera. Key ON, shift to Reverse. Black screen or "No Signal"? Set Rear Camera Type to 1080P and the rear frame rate to AHD 30 FPS.
  3. Climate. Open Car Model AC through Factory Settings (path below). Choose Auto AC for dual-zone automatic or Manual AC for single-zone, then tap OK to reboot.
  4. Wireless CarPlay / Android Auto. In CarAuto, tap the gear and enable Allow AutoBoot. Pair the phone to the radio's ID (e.g., 645F) under Settings → Bluetooth → Pair New Device.

Ford F-150: pre-install audit

Most comebacks start with the wrong harness. Confirm the audio system and vehicle before you start disassembly.

Applies to

Ford F-150, with harness notes for 2022+ Super Duty. Covered across Modules 6–9.

Tools

7 mm socket/wrench and a plastic trim tool.

The blue mini USB test. Look at the back of the factory radio chassis. A blue mini USB port means the truck has a Bang & Olufsen (B&O) system and needs the LinksWell B&O harness.
Install video: Watch the LinksWell install video for this vehicle at linkswellinc.com/support before your first install.

Why it matters

With a standard harness in a B&O truck, the screen powers up and works normally, but audio only plays through the tweeters. The factory amp isn't being triggered correctly.

What's in the box

Standard vehicle kits are all-inclusive: CANbus modules, dash bezel/trim, and the main wiring harnesses. Special cases like B&O and the touchscreen 360 retention module may need extra parts, so confirm them before the customer's appointment.

Ford F-150: dash disassembly

XL install sequence. Tools: 7 mm socket/wrench and a plastic trim tool.

  1. Top dash tray. Pop the center speaker grille (if equipped) to reach two 7 mm screws. Remove them and unclip the tray.
  2. AC bezel. Remove the two 7 mm screws behind the tray and unclip the AC control bezel.
  3. Transfer clips. Move every orange metal clip from the factory bezel to the new LinksWell bezel.
  4. Screen and chassis. Remove the six 7 mm screws holding the factory screen and the four holding the lower radio chassis/CD player.
  5. Relocate switches. The hazard and traction switches move from the top of the bezel to the bottom, where the CD player was. Split the factory harness tape behind the dash to get enough slack, then pull the switch harness down.
Before you put the factory chassis aside, check it for the blue mini USB port if you haven't already.

On the XL install, where do the hazard and traction switches go?

They move to the bottom opening. Split the factory tape behind the dash to extend the harness.

Module 9 · Ford

Ford F-150: software setup & hand-off

Program HVAC

Settings → System (top right) → Settings Guide → Car Model AC

GM trucks use different climate options (Auto AC / Manual AC). See Modules 4 and 5.

  1. Select the vehicle model (e.g., Ford F-150).
  2. Choose the climate type: Low = single-zone, High = dual-zone (separate driver/passenger temps).
  3. Choose the sub-model: Other for most trucks, With Touch 360 if the retention module is installed, 2013 for older F-150s.
  4. Select Current and tap OK. The unit saves and reboots on its own.

Turn on comfort features

Settings → System → Settings Guide → Seat and Wheel

After the reboot, toggle on whatever the truck has: heated seats, cooled seats, heated steering wheel. Their icons then show on the climate screen.

Camera switches

Settings → System → Settings Guide → Camera Switch
Never turn on both 360 and Multi. The unit can't tell which signal to decode, and the cameras stop working. With the 360 retention module: 360 on, Multi off.

Customer hand-off (all vehicles): Quick Boot fail-safe

To start almost instantly, the unit sleeps instead of fully shutting down. To keep memory from clogging up and crashing, it does a full cold reboot every 15–20 ignition cycles.

Say it like this: "Most of the time the screen comes on right away. Every 15 to 20 starts, it'll take a little longer because it's doing a full restart to clear its memory. That's normal, not a problem."

Also cover: wireless CarPlay/AA setup, that the factory USB ports are for media only, and warranty length (2 years XL/Gen 6, 1 year LITE 2).

The truck has separate driver and passenger temperature controls. Which climate type?

Knowledge check

Cameras stopped working after setup. 360 and Multi are both on. What's the fix?

Only one can be active. With a 360 setup, keep 360 on and Multi off.
